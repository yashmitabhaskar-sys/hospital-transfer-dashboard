ReferralLoop is a healthcare coordination platform that connects hospitals, clinics, laboratories, pharmacies, and patients to improve continuity of care. It maintains standardized patient medical histories, including diagnoses, test results, prescriptions, medications, and treatment outcomes.

Key Features:
Patient Access: Patients can securely view their medical history, upload previous prescriptions and test reports, track referrals, and access their treatment and follow-up information.
Medical History Management: Maintains a chronological record of diagnoses, investigations, medications, and treatment outcomes.
Referral and Transfer Tracking: Generates unique referral IDs and tracks transfers between healthcare facilities.
Handover Acknowledgment: Records when the receiving facility receives and accepts a referral, along with assigned follow-up responsibilities.
Missing-Information Checks: Flags incomplete medical records and handover details.
Repeat-Test Review: Identifies potentially duplicate investigations so clinicians can review previous results before ordering new tests.
Medication Reconciliation: Highlights discrepancies between recorded prescriptions, administered medicines, and dispensing records.
Event History and Follow-up Tracking: Maintains a timeline of referral activities and tracks pending actions until their outcomes are documented.

How It Works: The sending hospital creates a referral and attaches the patient's relevant medical records. ReferralLoop generates a unique tracking ID and forwards the handover to the receiving facility. The receiving team reviews the records, acknowledges receipt, checks for missing information, and documents the next steps. Patients can access their authorized records and follow-up information through the patient interface, while healthcare professionals access relevant information according to their permissions. Previous test results and medication records support clinical review, helping reduce avoidable delays and potentially unnecessary repeat investigations.

By bringing patients, medical records, and referral workflows together, ReferralLoop aims to improve transparency, accountability, and coordination across healthcare providers while keeping patients informed about their care.
